Where Do I See Myself in 4 Years?

In four years, I see myself pursuing a master's degree in cybersecurity while building on my background in game design and development. I want to focus on securing interactive systems, such as protecting online games from cheating, account theft, and network attacks. My long-term goal is to become a security engineer who makes digital worlds safer for the players who enjoy them.
